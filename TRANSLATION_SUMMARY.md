## Translation approach

### Quality standards

- Simple, international English
- Technical and sober tone
- No marketing jargon
- Comprehensible by humans AND AI
- Faithful translation (no philosophical reformulation)

### Translation choices

- "Vérification" → "Check" (not "verification")
- "Disponibilité" → "Availability"
- "Outil" → "Tool"
- "Brique universelle" → "Universal building block"
- "Indicateur technique" → "Technical indicator"
